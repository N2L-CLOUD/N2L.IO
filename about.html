The Vision Behind N2L

We are living through a fundamental inflection point. The Fourth Industrial Revolution is not coming — it is here. As artificial intelligence scales across compute, spatial reasoning, and agentic workflows, the bottleneck is no longer raw intelligence, but the systems that map, translate, and execute on that intelligence.

Neural Net Labs (N2L.dev) was founded by Herb Chiposi as a dedicated applied AI innovation lab. Rather than building incremental software, N2L focuses on three core pillars:

  • Applied Intelligence Lab: Engineering autonomous, agentic platforms like Anubis Agentic Research (cross-domain scientific problem solving) and VectorScope Cyber (real-time AI threat monitoring).
  • Spatial Intelligence Lab: Converting orbital telematics, satellite feeds, and 3D astronomical mechanics into interactive world models like Satalytics and Orbitarium.
  • Media Lab & TekBeacon: Mapping the technologies, companies, compute economics, and breakthroughs shaping our near-future reality via TekBeacon and AI-native creative media like Probable Sounds.

Life's Work & Execution

For Herb Chiposi, N2L is a long-term commitment to engineering foundational technology that empowers researchers, analysts, and builders. By combining agentic architecture with real-world domain models, N2L bridges the gap between frontier AI research and operational reality.
